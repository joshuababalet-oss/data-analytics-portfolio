# Big Store Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

**Tools:** Excel, Power BI, Pivot Tables, DAX  
**Data:** 9,000+ anonymized retail transactions  
**Date:** 2025

### Project Goal
Identify top-performing product categories, regions, and discount strategies. Find profit leakage points for a retail chain.

### Key Findings
 **Technology category** drove 50% of total profit despite being 35% of sales

### Dashboard Features
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Profit
- Sales vs Profit trend by month and year
- Profit by Category and Region bar charts  
- Interactive year/category slicers
